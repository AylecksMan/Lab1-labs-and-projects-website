Eamusement

Info about eamusement cards!

Eamusement cards are used to store your game data and profile from game to game. This includes your username, scores set on each machine, and arcade location history.

Alongside this, you are able to use the eamusement card on Konami's official website to register your card, view scores, or even download and play at home versions of your favorite rhythm games!

I mainly talk about Konami's game card, however there are other compatible game cards produced by other companies like SEGA, or Bandai Namco. These cards all function similarly.
